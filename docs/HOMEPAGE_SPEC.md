# FlowvaEditor homepage and category selection specification

## Purpose

Provide a guest-friendly starting point for the six creative workspaces, local projects/assets and real FlowvaMarket template discovery. Homepage presentation must reflect actual implementation status.

## Six workspaces

1. Graphic Design — active creation presets; shared canvas engine today, specialist toolset to be completed.
2. Social Media — active digital/social presets; platform-aware safe zones, variants and batch workflows are roadmap work.
3. Documents & Publishing — active single-page presets today; not yet a complete multi-page document editor.
4. Logo & Branding — active logo/app-icon presets today; advanced vector editing is not yet implied.
5. Video & Motion — active timeline-enabled presets; browser codec/export verification and workflow improvements remain.
6. 3D & Models — roadmap/coming soon; no 3D editor in current baseline.

## Homepage behaviour
- Guest visitors can browse and use local creation without an editor login.
- Category selection opens the relevant preset flow and carries workspace context into the editor.
- 3D remains visibly planned until a real module is implemented.
- Public Market templates come only from the approved FlowvaMarket catalogue endpoint; never seed fake production templates.
- Get opens the exact selected listing's canonical Market product page.
- Purchased items appear only from authenticated Market purchase data.
- Recent Projects section is hidden when empty; local activity is not proof of Market ownership.
- Local import/assets and local projects remain usable when Market is unavailable.

## Out of scope for homepage
- Implementing authentication, checkout, purchase ownership, creator payouts or moderation.
- Claiming every workspace has a specialist module before it exists.
- Adding fake templates or placeholder Edit/Publish success actions.
